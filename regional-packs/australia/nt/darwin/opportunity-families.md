# Darwin initial opportunity families

These are planning categories, not promises of fish or fixed spots.

## ESTUARY_CREEK
Typical objectives: barramundi/threadfin or general estuary opportunity. Candidate reasoning may consider creek mouths, drains, channel edges and adjacent structure. Access/tide dependencies can be material. Generated areas remain hypotheses.

## REEF_DEMERSAL
Typical objectives: mixed reef/demersal species around official artificial reefs or supported structure. An official reef coordinate is an `OFFICIAL_LOCATION`; fish-holding position around it remains observational/hypothesis territory. Range/equipment requirements must be evaluated rather than inferred.

## PELAGIC_OFFSHORE
Typical objectives: pelagics/FAD/offshore opportunities. Withhold operational options when usable fuel/range, critical vessel capability or current conditions needed for admission are unknown. Do not infer capability from a 100 hp outboard or hull length alone.

## GENERAL_OPPORTUNITY
When target is unspecified, compare admitted families rather than selecting a species silently. Explain which extra fact would materially change the comparison.
