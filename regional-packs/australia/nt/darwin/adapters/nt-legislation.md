# NT Fisheries legislation reference procedure

Source ID: `AU-NT-LEG-FISHERIES-REGULATIONS`.

1. Open the registered NT Legislation fisheries regulations page.
2. Record `checked_at`, status/in-force state, reprint/version metadata and the exact provision/schedule relied on.
3. For legal effect prefer current in-force legislation over summary guidance.
4. If the site reports superseded/amended status that conflicts with a cached or guidance statement, return `CONFLICTING` until the current provision is resolved.
5. Do not present keeper/legal guidance as `VERIFIED_CURRENT` without this or another current authoritative legal source.
