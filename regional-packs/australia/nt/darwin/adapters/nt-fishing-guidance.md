# NT fishing guidance reference procedure

Source ID: `AU-NT-GOV-FISHING-GUIDANCE`.

Use NT.GOV.AU fishing guidance for current official summaries, measurement instructions and user-facing rule navigation. Record `checked_at` and source URL. Treat it as authoritative guidance but preserve the distinction between guidance and legislation. Do not cache/repackage restricted page content for commercial redistribution. If guidance and current legislation conflict, return `CONFLICTING` and prefer legislation for legal effect.
