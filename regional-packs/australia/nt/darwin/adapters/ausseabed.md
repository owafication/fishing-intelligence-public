# AusSeabed prospect-data procedure

Source ID: `AU-AUSSEABED-MARINE-DATA`.

1. Resolve a specific bathymetry/geomorphology dataset, not merely the portal landing page.
2. Capture dataset/custodian metadata, licence/attribution, spatial resolution and coverage limitations before deriving a prospect.
3. Preserve source resolution in output geometry and precision statement.
4. Generated output is a fishing search area/hypothesis, not a navigation route or verified hazard-avoidance product.
5. If dataset-specific rights or resolution cannot be established, do not promote it into reusable production prospect data.
