# Navigation and temporary restriction resolution

For vessel/kayak routes or launch/access plans, check whether current official navigation/access notices are material to the route.

Possible constraints include:
- notices to mariners / local notices;
- closed waters and exclusion zones;
- temporary works/dredging/construction;
- no-wake/speed restrictions;
- bridge/channel/ramp closures;
- event/security restrictions;
- hazards or route-specific operating notices from the competent authority.

Return source, geographic/temporal scope, verification state, effective period and route consequence. Do not turn a notice into certified navigation advice. A route candidate shown on a map must surface a known material current restriction alongside it.
