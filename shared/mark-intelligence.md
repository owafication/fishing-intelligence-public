# Private mark intelligence

## Purpose
Turn user-owned marks and off-trip intel into explainable planning evidence without turning a mark into public/community location data or letting catch counts dominate exposure.

## Mark evidence
A private mark/location may accumulate:
- user notes and dated intel;
- structure/bottom observations with source context;
- methods/presentations tried;
- accepted catches and blanks;
- effort/exposure;
- visit recency;
- environmental context snapshots;
- species-specific personal patterns/hypotheses.

Off-trip intel is an `OBSERVATION` with source/source context and does not become a confirmed site fact merely because it was recorded against a mark.

## Planning projection
`MarkIntelligence` is a derived projection. When comparing known marks, use effort-normalised personal evidence, context similarity, recency/freshness and material unknowns. Catch count alone is insufficient.

A historically productive mark must not automatically suppress an exploration option. Planning may compare `USER_MARK` with `GENERATED_PROSPECT` when both are admitted and relevant.

## Privacy
Mark geometry and derived patterns are `PRIVATE` by default and inherit restrictive privacy. Sharing a catch or metric never implies permission to disclose the mark.
