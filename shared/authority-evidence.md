# Authority, evidence and claim discipline

## Evidence classes
- `AUTHORITATIVE`: regulator, official access authority, official warning or equivalent authority.
- `OBSERVED`: what the user or an instrument actually recorded.
- `SUPPORTED`: credible fishing/scientific knowledge supported by sources but not directly observed by this user.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: prospective interpretation, proposed mechanism or untested relationship.

Never silently promote HYPOTHESIS -> SUPPORTED/OBSERVED/AUTHORITATIVE or OBSERVED -> universal fact.

## Personal learning states
Separate observation, derived pattern, hypothesis and established personal pattern. A catch is an observation, not a pattern. A repeated association is not automatically causal.

## Safety/feasibility boundary
Use `Operational Feasibility`, not “Safety Engine”.
Allowed conclusions include:
- plan exceeds the user's configured limit;
- official closure/warning applies;
- documented launch/retrieval constraint is not met;
- critical information could not be verified;
- one option is more sheltered/shorter/lower-travel than another when those facts are supported.

Do not conclude that a trip is “safe” or independently label one plan “safer”. Readiness and feasibility are not safety certification.

## Regulations
Current legal/keeper guidance must have a verified authoritative source. Regulation state is one of `VERIFIED_CURRENT | VERIFIED_AS_OF | STALE | CONFLICTING | UNAVAILABLE`.
If current authoritative verification fails, withhold definitive keeper/legal guidance rather than substituting model memory.

## Fishing outcomes
Do not manufacture numerical catch probabilities from weak evidence. Prefer transparent descriptions such as target-priority, robust-conditions, exploration, stronger/weaker personal evidence, or insufficient data.
