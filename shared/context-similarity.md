# Context similarity

Similarity is explainable and dimension-wise, not one opaque score.

Possible dimensions include target species, location/prospect class, season, tide stage/range, time of day/daylight, wind, water temperature, depth, structure, fishing method and presentation.

Each used dimension is `HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW | UNKNOWN` with an explanation and source/capture status. Missing dimensions remain unknown. A retrospective environmental reconstruction can participate but must retain that limitation.

The claim being tested determines which dimensions are material. Do not require irrelevant dimensions or silently omit a dimension central to the claim.
