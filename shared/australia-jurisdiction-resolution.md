# Australian jurisdiction resolution

Before legal/admission checks resolve physical jurisdiction, water regime, fishing mode, and any cross-jurisdiction or Commonwealth overlay.

One trip may have multiple rule layers. State fishing law may coexist with an Australian Marine Park zoning overlay. Apply all relevant hard constraints.

If jurisdiction is ambiguous near a border, seaward limit or impoundment boundary, do not guess. Return `PROVISIONAL` or `BLOCKED` for the dependent legal conclusion.

Tasmania requires explicit marine-vs-inland classification around seaward limits. WA requires bioregion/activity resolution where relevant.
