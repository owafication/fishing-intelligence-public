# Selection-bias tracking

Record, where known, why an attempt was selected: `USER_CHOICE | PLANNER_RECOMMENDATION | EXPLORATION | EXPERIMENT | OPPORTUNISTIC | UNKNOWN`.

If the planner repeatedly sends the angler only during one tide/condition, later catch history is policy-selected evidence. Flag material selection imbalance rather than treating the resulting catch distribution as an unbiased comparison.

Selection-bias flags reduce confidence; they do not erase the observations.
