# Personal learning contract

Fishing Intelligence learns from accepted fishing evidence, not from catch counts alone.

## Denominator first
Use `PROSPECT_ATTEMPT` and recorded effort as the preferred denominator. A catch distribution is not evidence of condition advantage until exposure under those conditions is considered. Example: 7 of 9 catches on falling water is weak evidence if roughly the same proportion of effort was spent on falling water.

## Population
Every pattern names its population: target species, area/prospect scope, method, time period and material context dimensions. Do not compare unlike fishing simply because records share a species name.

## Outputs
- observation: what happened;
- descriptive pattern: relationship in accepted records;
- hypothesis: proposed explanation/prediction;
- supported personal pattern: repeated relationship in this user's comparable evidence.

A supported personal pattern is not a universal biological rule and not automatically causal.

## Performance metrics
Use `personal-performance-metrics.md` for CPLH and other derived rates. A high rate is descriptive evidence for its named population, not a universal quality score or causal claim.
