# Pattern promotion and exposure correction

## Exposure correction
For comparisons such as tide stage, lure, location or method, compare outcomes relative to fishing exposure. Prefer catch-per-hour or another declared effort-normalized measure when duration/effort coverage is adequate. When effort is missing, keep the result descriptive and state the limitation.

## Default evidence gates
These are conservative software defaults, not biological laws:
- `INSUFFICIENT`: fewer than 3 comparable attempts, fewer than 2 independent trips, or <80% usable effort coverage for an effort-normalized claim.
- `EMERGING`: at least 3 comparable attempts across >=2 trips, adequate effort coverage, and a repeated association not wholly explained by one trip.
- `SUPPORTED`: normally requires >=12 comparable attempts across >=5 trips, >=90% effort coverage, repeated direction of association, and no single trip contributing >50% of usable effort.

A domain-specific evaluator may require stronger evidence. Never promote merely because a numerical threshold was crossed. Contradictory evidence, selection bias or poor comparability can keep a pattern lower.

## Causality
Observational association does not establish mechanism or causality. Explanations remain `HYPOTHESIS` unless stronger evidence justifies more.
