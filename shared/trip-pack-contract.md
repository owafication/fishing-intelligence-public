# Trip pack

A Trip Pack is a read-mostly snapshot for field reference. It is not a claim that all current-state information remains valid indefinitely.

Include the plan/fallback sequence, prospect geometry/precision, verification summary, privacy state and validity information for time-sensitive components.

Expired weather or access information does not erase the plan; it requires rechecking before dependent conclusions are treated as current. Link to external sources rather than copying whole external pages, maps, forecasts or tide tables into the pack.
