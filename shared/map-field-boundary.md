# Field map boundary

The map is for fishing context and field interaction, not certified marine navigation.

Display classes must remain distinguishable:
- `OFFICIAL_LOCATION`;
- `USER_MARK`;
- `GENERATED_PROSPECT`;
- legal/access exclusion overlays.

Generated prospects retain `navigation_suitable=false`. Approximate official reef positions remain approximate. Low-tide imagery/bathymetry keeps its source limitations.

The app may offer a **handoff** to an external/navigation system where available, but Fishing Intelligence does not convert non-navigational prospect geometry into navigation-safe routes or waypoints.


## Access-constraint display

A launch/access location displayed on a map or option list must surface any known material access constraint available to the plan (for example a current closure, tidal threshold, obstruction/bar warning or operating restriction). Do not let map inclusion imply operational endorsement when admission is provisional, withheld or constrained.
