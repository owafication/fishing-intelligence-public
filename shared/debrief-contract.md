# Trip debrief contract

A debrief compares plan and execution without equating fishing outcome with decision quality.

Record:
- planned vs actual launch/timing/prospect sequence;
- attempts made and effort;
- catches/blank effort;
- replans and why;
- material conditions that differed from the plan;
- data gaps;
- what evidence changed, if anything.

A blank trip does not by itself prove a plan was poor. A high catch count does not by itself prove a plan was good. Evaluate whether the plan respected verified constraints and whether assumptions/invalidation conditions behaved as expected separately from stochastic fishing outcome.
