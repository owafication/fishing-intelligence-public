# Species taxonomy resolution

Common fish names are not globally unique. Before binding rules to a species, resolve identity strongly enough for the consequential claim.

States:
`UNRESOLVED | COMMON_NAME_SCOPED | TAXON_RESOLVED | USER_CONFIRMED | AUTHORITY_CONFIRMED | CONFLICTING`.

Rules:
- Use location/water regime to narrow common-name ambiguity.
- Prefer an authoritative fisheries/taxonomic source when exact species identity changes size, bag, method, protected-status or season rules.
- An image-model suggestion remains proposed evidence until confirmed under `species-identification-input.md`.
- Never attach keeper/compliance advice to an ambiguous common name when plausible taxa have different rules.
- Preserve scientific name, authority/source and aliases when resolved; do not silently rewrite the user's terminology.
