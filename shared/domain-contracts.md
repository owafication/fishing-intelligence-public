# Fishing records

Fishing Intelligence may work with trips, sessions, attempts, catches, effort, equipment use, observations, prospects, locations, debriefs, patterns, hypotheses and active-trip state.

Records should carry enough identity, time, privacy and source context to support planning and analysis. Corrections should be explicit rather than silently changing a previously accepted fact.

Derived outputs such as trip packs, share packages, travel comparisons, mark intelligence, compliance summaries and performance metrics are decision-support outputs; they do not replace the underlying user observations.

Use ISO-8601 times with timezone where material, WGS84 unless the source declares otherwise, and preserve source precision/datum when relevant. Unknown remains unknown.
