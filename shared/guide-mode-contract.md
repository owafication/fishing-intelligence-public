# Guide / charter operating profile

Guide mode optimises the **fishing operation**, not bookings, payments or CRM.

A `GuideTripContext` can include group size, client fishing experience, mobility/access needs supplied by the operator, target/experience objective, available time, equipment allocation, travel tolerance, comfort preferences and variety/repeat-water preferences.

Client context is trip input. It does not overwrite the guide/owner fishing profile and is not silently promoted into durable personal data.

The planner may compare objectives such as catch opportunity, lower travel, sheltered water, variety, teaching suitability or exploration when supported. It must not label a plan safe or infer sensitive client attributes.
