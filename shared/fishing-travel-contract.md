# Fishing travel planning

Fishing travel compares destinations/regions using regional-pack readiness, target opportunities, season/window, access mode, equipment/logistics and legal dependencies.

It may say a destination is only `REFERENCE_READY` and therefore requires more local verification before an operational fishing plan. It must not inflate every regional pack to the same evidence depth.

Accommodation, flights, vehicle rental, bookings and payments are outside Fishing Intelligence. Return a concise fishing/logistics requirement summary rather than claiming that a transaction was performed.
