# Travel and logistics comparison contract

Fishing quality is not the only option cost. When alternatives differ materially in travel, towing, access timing or setup burden, the planner must expose the trade-off rather than silently choosing the biologically attractive option.

## Material logistics

Consider, where relevant and available:
- origin-to-launch/shore-access travel distance and time;
- towing/road/access constraints;
- launch/facility opening times or booking/access windows;
- travel time consumed from the user's available fishing window;
- fuel/range implications when actually known or verifiable;
- daylight/night-operation consequences;
- relocation cost when changing between options during the same trip.

Do not invent travel time, distance, fuel burn or range. If a logistics fact could change which option is feasible and is not verified, preserve it as an unknown or conditional trade-off. If unverified travel/setup time could consume the user's available fishing window or violate a hard return/departure limit, withhold that option until the constraint is resolved rather than ranking it optimistically.

A farther option may still be preferred, but the user-visible comparison should explain what additional travel/setup burden buys in fishing or operational terms.
