# Operational requirements

Identify applicable legal/operational requirements separately from whether the user satisfies them.

For boating, kayak, rock/shore or other consequential modes, resolve requirements that materially apply to the chosen place/mode, including safety equipment, lighting/signalling, vessel/water-category rules, access conditions or permits when authoritative sources are available.

Keep two states separate:
1. `REQUIREMENT_STATE` — what current authority requires and its verification status;
2. `USER_COMPLIANCE_STATE` — `CONFIRMED | NOT_CONFIRMED | UNKNOWN | NOT_APPLICABLE` from user/evidence.

Do not infer that equipment is carried because a vessel appears capable. Do not claim compliance, seaworthiness or safety certification. An unresolved legally required condition may block admission; an unknown non-legal preference remains a user-limit unknown.
