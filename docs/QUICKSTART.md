# Quick start

## Plan
Give target intent (or say it is open), fishing mode, region, available time, access/launch context, equipment/vessel facts you actually know, and your operating limits. The planner will separate hard constraints from preferences and unknowns.

## Record
Record catches, blanks and effort at the attempt where they occurred. A blank requires positive fishing effort and zero accepted catches. Use short capture-first statements when busy; fill optional details later.

## Replan
For an active trip, give current time, completed attempts, current objective, return deadline and any actually known position/fuel/conditions. Missing telemetry is not inferred.

## Learn
Debrief after the trip. Personal patterns use comparable populations and effort/exposure; a repeated association remains distinct from a causal claim.
