# Contributing

Contributions, bug reports and regional-source improvements are welcome.

## Before submitting

- Keep examples and fixtures synthetic.
- Do not include private fishing locations, credentials, personal data or copied third-party pages/datasets.
- Prefer links to current authoritative sources instead of embedding mutable legal, weather, tide or access content.
- Preserve the product boundaries: Fishing Intelligence is decision support, not certified navigation or safety certification.
